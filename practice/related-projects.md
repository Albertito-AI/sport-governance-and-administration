# Teaching with the Atlas projects

Each link is paired with a specific exercise a club, league or seminar group can try. Use fictional or authorised extracts when running public examples. Each project states its own scope and terms.

| Project | Application | Exercise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Atlas Agent School](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/atlas-agent-school-public) | Agent training and assessment | Assign a records agent, a finance reviewer and an athlete representative different evidence in the venue exercise. Compare their answers before a director signs. |
| [Atlas Open Mathematics](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/atlas-open-mathematics) | Allocation, uncertainty and reproducibility | Reproduce the feasibility calculation under both weather scenarios. Derive the £101 change that eliminates the last affordable plan. |
| [Tallystick](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/tallystick) | Results, integrity and provenance | Trace a contested scoring observation to the official correction. Have the registrar account for both versions before releasing a table. |
| [RegNav Source Watch](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/regnav-source-watch) | Rules and source changes | Track a nomination policy or grant condition. A changed source opens a review for the officer who relies on that clause. |
| [Atlas Opsbook](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/atlas-opsbook) | Staff and volunteer continuity | Give the incoming event director a tested handover rule after an accessible transfer fails. Record who verified the change and when it expires. |
| [Atlas Reweave](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/atlas-reweave) | Dependencies and revisions | Reopen an accepted venue comparison when a transport quote or approval changes. Keep the original calculation and the fresh authority decision. |
| [Atlas Research Library](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/atlas-research-library-public) | Evidence curation | Use the public evidence schema to catalogue a tribunal decision, a regulator’s order and a sponsor statement with dates and distinct evidentiary weight. |
| [Haze Lens](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/haze-lens) | Environment and event continuity | Compare a dated Singapore haze observation with the authorised event advisory. Record the next reassessment and the officer who can change the programme. |
| [EVT-Albert](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/evtalbert) | Athlete voice and data stewardship | Use synthetic training diary entries to critique who can see an athlete’s self-report. Ask an athlete representative to approve the purpose and retention period. |
| [Piepowder](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/piepowder) | Contract and procurement evidence | Apply an explicit acceptance test to a supplier’s accessibility deliverable. Keep evidence of delivery apart from the officer’s authority to release payment. |
| [Court Submission Integrity](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/court-submission-integrity) | Appeals and record preparation | Preflight a fictional appeal bundle for missing annexes and unreliable authorities. The panel takes responsibility for jurisdiction and reasons. |
| [CohortFrame](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/cohortframe) | Volunteer induction and communications | Turn an approved scenario into a short accessible staff briefing. Verify the final transcript and consent before briefing officials. |

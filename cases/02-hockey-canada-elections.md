# Who gets onto the ballot?

Chapter case 02 · Chapter 2, *Boards, members and athlete voice* · Canada

## The public record

Thomas Cromwell’s governance review of Hockey Canada, dated 31 October 2022, examined who could propose candidates, who vetted them and who elected the board. It recommended an annually reviewed skills matrix, independent members forming a majority of the nominations committee and athlete participation in its work. Members would retain the capacity to propose candidates, with the committee assessing applicants for the ballot. On 17 December 2022 Hockey Canada reported that its members had elected a chair and eight directors proposed by the independent committee, for a special one-year term.

- [Thomas Cromwell, Hockey Canada Governance Review, Final Report, 31 October 2022, executive summary and discussion of the Nominating Committee](https://cdn.hockeycanada.ca/hockey-canada/Corporate/action-plan/downloads/2022-hockey-canada-governance-review-final-report-e.pdf)
- [Hockey Canada, members elect the new board, 17 December 2022](https://www.hockeycanada.ca/en-ca/news/hockey-canadas-members-elect-new-board-of-directors-2022-corp)

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

In this fictional Harbour election the chair has received a slate from an influential club president and a request to move straight to a vote. Voting clubs disagree about whether an independent committee would open up elections or simply move the power to exclude candidates behind a closed door. As returning officer, advise which questions can be resolved at this meeting and which must return to the members.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Election rule in force | Every eligible candidate nominated by two voting member clubs by the published deadline goes on the ballot. The election chair can appoint a scrutineer. Only members can amend the election rule at a properly convened meeting. |
| Nominations | Two clubs have jointly nominated a former player before the deadline. Their emails establish the nominations. An outgoing director wants the candidate removed because the present skills matrix has no category for athlete experience. |
| Proxy file | Five forms presented by one club president appear on time. Two appoint different proxies for the same club, and the records omit times of receipt. The other three have separate signatures and identify different appointing clubs. |
| Independent scrutineer | The appointed scrutineer once stood for the board alongside the club president. A reserve scrutineer is available to check disputed appointments. The agenda contains an election and a discussion of next year’s rules. |

## Decisions due

1. Rule on the former player’s place on this ballot. Draft a minute that distinguishes verification of an existing eligibility condition from the introduction of a new criterion.
2. Design a proxy reconciliation table. For each challenged appointment state what evidence you will seek, who will decide it, how its holder can respond and when a ruling could affect the count.
3. Propose two election reforms for the next cycle. Preserve a route for members to put forward candidates and specify how athletes can influence the criteria, challenge a procedural error and find out what changed.

## An agent assignment for the returning officer

Commission a comparison of proxy forms against the register as it stood on the qualifying date. The agent can flag duplicates and classifications that changed after notice. The officer contacts voters privately and records a ruling on each challenge before the count.

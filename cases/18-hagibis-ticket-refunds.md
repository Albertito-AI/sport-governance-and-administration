# Two matches off, four kinds of ticket

Cross-department simulation 18 · Chapter 8, *Facilities, procurement and events* · Japan

## The public record

On 10 October 2019 World Rugby and the Japan Rugby 2019 Organising Committee announced the cancellation of New Zealand v Italy and England v France in anticipation of Typhoon Hagibis. Tournament rules awarded each team two points for an unplayed pool match. Ticket holders were promised a refund of face value. A later venue and infrastructure assessment allowed some Sunday fixtures to proceed, while Namibia v Canada in Kamaishi was cancelled. The Saturday announcement therefore supplied a sporting and financial decision for named matches while leaving other fixtures open.

- [World Rugby, Typhoon Hagibis update and ticket refund terms, 10 October 2019](https://www.world.rugby/news/505639/19?lang=en)
- [World Rugby, Sunday fixture and venue assessment update, 13 October 2019](https://www.world.rugby/news/510965/1013-v-v?lang=en)

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

In a fictional City Women’s League double-header, officials cancel Saturday’s match after the transport authority suspends its evening services. Sunday’s fixture remains under review. You lead the ticket office and must fund Saturday’s refunds while keeping the weekend’s accessible seating and companion arrangements intelligible.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Saturday sales ledger | Three hundred single-match tickets sold at £24 each. A further 80 weekend passes sold at £40 each, with the printed terms allocating £20 to each match. Thirty complimentary Saturday admissions have no face value. The figures exclude unredeemed sponsor allocations. |
| Reseller settlement | A ticket agent supplied 60 of the 300 single-match tickets. Payment records identify the ticket agent as merchant. Ten records in a merged file repeat the same order reference. It is unclear whether those repeats are duplicate imports or separate barcodes on one order. |
| Access and cash | Twelve Saturday customers separately booked accessible transport. An independent provider sold the transport. The league holds £9,000 in general cash and £2,500 in ring-fenced youth funding. The finance director alone can release payments. |

## Decisions due

1. Calculate the face-value liability on Saturday’s tickets, showing how the 60 reseller tickets, weekend passes, complimentary seats and restricted funds enter the calculation. Allocate responsibility for paying customers and reconciling the reseller settlement.
2. Design a reconciliation that an AI agent can run in parallel with the ticket office. Include a test that distinguishes repeat order references from repeat ticket entitlements, a numerical acceptance criterion, and a route for exceptions.
3. Send an operational instruction for the Saturday cancellation and a separate Sunday holding notice. Assign a named role to contact accessible-transport customers, brief volunteers and settle package questions.

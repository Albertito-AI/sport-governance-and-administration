# What should a board spend after the bid has gone wrong?

Chapter case 16 · Chapter 16, *Evaluating a season and defending the next budget* · Victoria, Australia

## The public record

Victoria signed the host contract for the 2026 Commonwealth Games in April 2022, announced its withdrawal in July 2023 and finalised it the following month. The Victorian Auditor-General’s report, tabled on 20 March 2024, put the cost of bidding, planning and withdrawal at over A$589 million, including the A$380 million cancellation settlement. It found that the initial budget was unrealistically low and the A$6.9 billion estimate publicly announced on withdrawal was overstated. The March 2022 business case included contested health and civic-pride benefits and relied on assumptions the audit considered insufficient for an informed decision.

- [Victorian Auditor-General’s Office, Withdrawal from 2026 Commonwealth Games (20 March 2024), ‘What we found’ and section 3, ‘Advice to the government’](https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/report/withdrawal-2026-commonwealth-games)
- [Victorian Auditor-General’s Office, official presentation transcript accompanying the March 2024 audit](https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-24/Commonwealth%20Games%202026/20240320_Withdrawal-from-2026-Commonwealth-Games-video-transcript.pdf)

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

In this fictional Australian municipal exercise, a council-owned sport trust must recommend whether to deliver a regional festival or cancel and fund a neighbourhood season. A sponsor is pressing for an announcement. The council, transport service and trust have each prepared figures on a different basis.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Already spent | A$180,000 has been paid for planning and is irrecoverable under either option. No part of this amount is included in the following estimates. |
| Deliver the festival | Further event contracts total A$260,000. A separate municipal transport allocation would cover an additional A$50,000 to A$95,000. Finance has checked these are distinct costs. |
| Withdraw | The quoted cancellation settlement is A$70,000 if accepted by Friday. A separate neighbourhood season would cost A$110,000. Both figures are prospective and neither includes past planning expenditure. |
| Claimed legacy | The draft credits the festival with annual health savings from new participants. No one has identified who is new or who would have been active anyway. A council analyst can obtain booking baselines in four weeks. |

## Decisions due

1. Reconcile historic spending and future public cost on one sheet. Show the range for continuing and the costs of cancellation with and without the neighbourhood season. Make the treatment of the transport allocation visible.
2. Draft a recommendation to the council before Friday. Describe one non-financial reason to favour each feasible option, the uncertain benefit with greatest effect and the official authorised to accept the settlement.
3. Give the scrutiny committee a plan for checking the legacy claim. Specify the baseline, comparison, data owner and publication date, and identify how residents would learn which activities survive an early exit.

## An agent assignment for the board and funder

Use an agent to keep the original cost estimate, revised commitments and cost of withdrawal in separate columns. Invite the host community to challenge the assumptions before the board chooses. A number labelled ‘spent’ needs a payment date and a purpose.

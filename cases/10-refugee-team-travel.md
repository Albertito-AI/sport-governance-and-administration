# Selected for the team, still waiting to travel

Chapter case 10 · Chapter 10, *High performance and athlete pathways* · Olympic Movement and European Union

## The public record

The International Olympic Committee announced 36 athletes for its Paris Refugee Olympic Team on 2 May 2024. At the time of the announcement, the athletes trained under 15 host national Olympic committees and were to compete across 12 sports. Its Executive Board subsequently invited runner Dominic Lobalu after he had achieved the qualifying standard and his refugee status in Switzerland had been verified. Separate arrangements governed entry to the host state. Annex XI, articles 3–5, of the EU Visa Code in force for the Games set conditions for visas and provided for organising committees to forward applications to the designated visa authorities. These records distinguish a sporting invitation, entry to competition and a decision by the competent border or visa authority.

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

You manage a fictional international development squad travelling to an invitational camp. One selected athlete trains in a host country and travels on a refugee travel document. The private invitational has no Olympic accreditation or Games visa procedure. The camp has limited coached places, the athlete has consented to confidential liaison, and the organiser has agreed to keep one place open until Thursday.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Sporting position | The panel selected the athlete on the published standard. Only the panel can alter the selection. A reserve has been named and needs 24 hours to arrange release from work. |
| Travel position | Travel staff have an invitation and the athlete’s confirmation that the travel document is current. They have received no authoritative advice about the proposed transit or the athlete’s return to the country where they train. |
| Fare and delegation | A non-refundable fare of €430 expires at 15.00 today. A €690 flexible fare can be held until Thursday noon for €35, credited against that fare. The team manager may spend €500 per trip. The performance director can approve a higher sum. The camp will accept an alternative routing if its arrival deadline is met. |
| Athlete’s wishes | The athlete wants an interpreter for a private call and asks that their immigration history be kept out of email to the full squad. The press office has scheduled individual profiles for tomorrow. |

## Decisions due

1. Write a movement decision sheet showing who can confirm selection, camp entry, transit requirements and the return journey. Identify the evidence each recipient needs and what the coach can be told now.
2. Compare the fare options at 15.00, including the cost at risk and the authorisation needed. Set a decision time that leaves the reserve a workable opportunity without withdrawing the selected athlete prematurely.
3. Draft a confidential update for the athlete and a separate message suitable for the squad. Assign responsibility for interpretation, travel advice, renewed contact with the camp and handling the athlete’s preference about publicity.

## An agent assignment for the chef de mission

Use an accreditation agent to identify expiring deadlines across delegations and request confirmation through the authorised liaison. A ticket purchase can wait for documentary clearance. Individual travel status belongs with those assisting the athlete, with an accessible route to correct the record.

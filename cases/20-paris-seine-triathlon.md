# Who can clear the swim?

Cross-department simulation 20 · Chapter 15, *Data, climate and operational risk* · France

## The public record

At a 03.30 meeting on 30 July 2024, Paris 2024, World Triathlon, its technical and medical delegates and public authorities considered water-quality evidence for the Seine. The men’s Olympic triathlon, originally due at 08.00 that morning, was postponed. World Triathlon announced a revised start for the following day, subject to forthcoming evidence satisfying its swimming requirements, and retained 2 August as a contingency. The men’s and women’s individual events both took place on 31 July.

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

Borough Sport Trust operates a fictional open-water festival. As operations director you have a reserve window on a school day, a road closure that expires at noon and a live sponsor broadcast. The responsible officers need a timetable that shows exactly when permission to race can be given and who may communicate it.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Signed event plan | The environmental authority supplies its dated assessment. A start requires separate sign-off from the medical lead and technical delegate. Either can withhold approval. The event director can postpone and the city alone can extend the road order. |
| Evidence at 05.20 | The current assessment covers only part of the course. Yesterday’s signed sheet remains visible in the shared folder. A team dashboard has imported that sheet and displays a green status. The record owner expects a further assessment at 06.15. |
| Dependencies | Accessible shuttle providers need 45 minutes’ notice and the school transport coordinator needs an agreed return window. Television wants confirmation at 05.45. Only the steward supervisor can confirm replacement staffing. |

## Decisions due

1. Build a signed decision log for 05.20, 05.45 and 06.15. Record which evidence applies to which course and date, who can withhold a start, and which independent authorisations remain open.
2. Specify a read-only evidence agent that attaches the owner, effective date and coverage to each document, highlights superseded records and pages the responsible officer. Test it on yesterday’s approval and today’s partial assessment.
3. Write one instruction for athletes and access providers during the hold and a second for broadcasters. Work out the latest shuttle notice for each proposed start and identify who can activate the reserve arrangements.

# A federation is suspended. Who can still play?

Chapter case 01 · Chapter 1, *Authority and the organisation of sport* · Sri Lanka and international cricket

## The public record

On 10 November 2023 the International Cricket Council suspended Sri Lanka Cricket, citing interference with its obligation to govern the sport autonomously. The first notice left the conditions of suspension open. On 21 November, after hearing from Sri Lanka Cricket, the ICC Board permitted international participation, retained control over ICC funding to the member and transferred the 2024 men’s under-19 World Cup to South Africa. The Board lifted the suspension on 28 January 2024. The three notices matter because an athlete, a broadcaster and a creditor each had a different question about the same sanction.

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

You are Harbour Sport Federation’s secretary. In this fictional exercise Harbour’s international federation suspended its membership yesterday. A junior team travels in nine days. The host city wants a public assurance by noon that its separate tournament will go ahead. Families have already paid for rail travel. Give the chair a recommendation before the morning meeting ends.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Suspension notice | The message suspends membership immediately and promises conditions within five working days. It says nothing about team entry, accreditation, funding or the host award. |
| Travel and hosting | The airline will hold seats until 16.00 for a refundable £400. The hotel wants a non-refundable £3,200 by noon. Only the international federation can confirm entries and host appointments. |
| Grant letter | The public grant pays for junior travel. Its agreement requires Harbour to notify the agency of a change in international recognition within two working days. Only the agency may approve a change of purpose. |
| The bank mandate | The chief executive can approve a refundable hold within the travel budget. Non-refundable expenditure above £2,000 requires board approval and a second authorised signatory. |

## Decisions due

1. Write the four questions that require a ruling, naming the authority that can answer each. Identify what Harbour may still decide today and who will record the incoming decisions.
2. Minute the choice on the seats and hotel, showing the maximum sum at risk and the authority for each commitment. Set a time for calling families even if the governing body has yet to reply.
3. Draft a 90-word joint briefing for athletes and the city that makes clear what each can rely on today. Give the grant manager a separate instruction with an owner and deadline.

## An agent assignment for the federation secretary

Have the agent assemble the current membership notice, entry rules and grant conditions in a dated register. A suspension alert should ask three different owners to confirm entry, hosting and money. Only the holder of each power can settle the relevant line.

# A Buenos Aires final moves to Madrid

Cross-department simulation 22 · Chapter 6, *Leagues, markets and media rights* · Argentina and Spain

## The public record

The second leg of the 2018 Copa Libertadores final between River Plate and Boca Juniors was postponed after Boca’s team bus was attacked in Buenos Aires. CONMEBOL announced that the game would take place in Madrid. On 8 December the Court of Arbitration for Sport rejected Boca’s request for provisional measures suspending the match. The game was played on 9 December. In the later award of 4 February 2020 the panel found River Plate responsible for disciplinary violations and ordered two future home Libertadores matches behind closed doors. The urgent application and the final ruling therefore answered different questions.

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

The fictional City Women’s League is invited to take its final abroad after its host venue becomes unavailable. The league board retains the venue decision. A club has requested a temporary stay from the competition appeals panel. Supporter groups say the proposed seat allocation rewards those who can fund the trip themselves.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Relocation offer | The alternate host waives the stadium fee. The net cost of team travel and additional broadcast production is £34,000, payable by the league. Its available contingency is £48,000, with no permission to use restricted development grants. |
| Access record | Of 1,800 existing ticket holders, 520 replied to a survey and 160 of those said they could fund the trip. The supporters’ liaison can also reach organised groups, cash purchasers, disabled supporters and companion ticket holders. A domestic ground is available six days later. |
| Decision timetable | The alternate host’s offer expires at 18.00. The panel promises an urgent decision by 17.30. The television contract allows a change of date by written agreement, which has yet to be sought. The original ticket terms promise a refund on relocation. |

## Decisions due

1. Compare the foreign and delayed domestic venues. State what the survey can establish, devise a rapid check with missing supporter groups and identify who can bind the broadcaster, the host and each club.
2. Prepare two conditional board resolutions for the panel’s possible orders. Include who may incur costs, how the £14,000 residual contingency would be protected if the offer is accepted, and an alternative if a refund reserve is insufficient.
3. Design an AI-assisted comparison of eligibility, ticket entitlements and communication preferences using a de-identified extract. Require a human to test accessible seats and companion links, sign the refund schedule and approve messages in both host languages.

# A team-selection complaint reaches the wrong forum

Cross-department simulation 21 · Chapter 14, *Selection, discipline and disputes* · Kenya

## The public record

In Ongwenyi v Royal Nairobi Golf Club [2026] KEHC 1187 (KLR), a member’s grievances included the selection of the club’s women’s team. The club challenged the Sports Disputes Tribunal’s ruling that it could hear the complaint. On 10 February 2026 the High Court quashed the tribunal proceedings and its ruling. Addressing section 58 of the Sports Act, the Court considered the nature of the dispute, the club’s status and the absence of a constitutional or agreed referral. It expressly left the underlying dispute unresolved and the member free to pursue other remedies before a competent forum.

- [High Court of Kenya, Ongwenyi v Royal Nairobi Golf Club [2026] KEHC 1187 (KLR), especially paras 60–63](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2026/1187/eng%402026-02-10/source.pdf)
- [Kenya, Sports Act 2013, s 58, jurisdiction of the Sports Disputes Tribunal](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2013/25/eng%402022-12-31/source.pdf)

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

In a fictional Kenyan members’ club, a player omitted from a club side submits a complaint about access to selection trials. The team travels in four days. As club secretary you must preserve an effective remedy while identifying the rules governing this particular team, the club’s registration and the possible review bodies.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Documents at reception | The file contains a current club constitution and a receipt for affiliation fees paid to a national body. The file contains no certificate of registration under the Sports Act. Clause 12 authorises an independent internal review within two working days. |
| Selection and communications | The selection list is signed by the club captain. An undated website FAQ describes all selection as appealable to the sports tribunal. The constitution names no external sports tribunal. No agreement to a referral has been produced. |
| Conflict and remedy | The designated reviewer helped design the trial. An alternate reviewer is available. A reserve player’s place could be affected, and the competition organiser can hold the entry until noon tomorrow if asked today. |

## Decisions due

1. Write a forum memorandum distinguishing a club decision, a decision of a national sports organisation and any consensual reference. Identify what each of the constitution, affiliation receipt, registration certificate and referral agreement could prove.
2. Draft directions that preserve the entry deadline and a fair hearing. Deal with the reviewer’s involvement, prompt disclosure, the reserve player’s opportunity to respond and reasons for any interim arrangement.
3. Give a document-retrieval agent a bounded search for the signed rules, edition dates and any agreement. Write an audit test in which the FAQ conflicts with the constitution and assign a person to check the original instruments before sending the parties any advice.

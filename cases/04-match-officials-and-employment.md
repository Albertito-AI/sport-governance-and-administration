# What does the coach’s working week establish?

Chapter case 04 · Chapter 4, *People, work and voluntary clubs* · England and Wales; United Kingdom tax proceedings

## The public record

In HMRC v Professional Game Match Officials Ltd [2024] UKSC 29, the Supreme Court held that individual match contracts with National Group football referees met the minimum requirements of mutual obligation and control. It remitted the overall assessment for income tax and National Insurance purposes. On 1 May 2026 the First-tier Tribunal allowed PGMOL’s appeals and held that the individual engagements were contracts for services. The freedom to decline appointments, the character of the assessment system and the referees’ economic circumstances all informed the tribunal’s evaluation. The dispute concerned fees paid in the 2014/15 and 2015/16 tax years.

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

Foundry Athletic Club has described its founder-coach’s payments as an honorarium for three seasons. The treasurer leaves in a fortnight and the founder has asked for time away. You are the incoming chair. Arrange cover for junior training and commission an assessment of the engagement that reconciles the paper agreement with what happened at the club.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Agreement | The coach is paid £450 a week during term. The agreement allows the coach to propose a suitably qualified substitute. It calls the arrangement voluntary and is silent about preparation and competition travel. |
| Rota and correspondence | For twelve weeks the committee set 30 hours weekly, including six hours preparing session plans. The coach requested permission to miss two evenings. The chair refused one request, and the club found cover on the other evening. |
| Bank and expenses | The same £450 was paid each term week. Receipted competition-travel costs were reimbursed separately. No record shows a substitute paid by the coach. The coach trades independently during the summer. |
| Athlete welfare and cover | The assistant can supervise adults independently. The club’s junior-session policy requires a second, appropriately cleared adult. Two junior sessions are due during the founder’s leave. |

## Decisions due

1. Write a two-column brief for payroll and the club’s adviser comparing the referees’ match engagements with this coaching relationship. Include one fact favouring each possible characterisation and one gap that could change your view.
2. Prepare a 48-hour duty roster that respects the coach’s leave and the junior-session policy. Identify the person who will tell families about any change and make the rota available to the incoming treasurer without disclosing unnecessary personal details.
3. In a minute for the committee, assign responsibility for a tax-status review and a separate assessment of employment rights, pay and records. Specify the periods to examine, the evidence to preserve and how to protect payment already earned.

## An agent assignment for the club chair and treasurer

Ask an agent to reconcile the rota, invoices, cancellation messages and actual substitutions. Give the coach a chance to correct that chronology. The chair can then commission a status assessment and cost the staffing arrangement that the club actually runs.

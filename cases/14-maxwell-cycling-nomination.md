# A nomination appeal with the Olympic entry deadline approaching

Chapter case 14 · Chapter 14, *Selection, discipline and disputes* · New Zealand

## The public record

On 4 July 2024 the Sports Tribunal of New Zealand allowed Samara (Sammie) Maxwell’s appeal against Cycling New Zealand’s refusal to nominate her for the women’s mountain biking event in Paris. Its reasons, dated 11 July, identified two successful grounds. The federation had applied its published nomination criteria incorrectly, and the evidence could not reasonably support its decision. The panel had relied on outdated or inaccurate medical material and the athlete had lacked an opportunity to address an adverse report. Under rule 49(b)(i) the Tribunal nominated Maxwell itself because there was too little time for effective reconsideration. Final selection belonged to the New Zealand Olympic Committee.

- [Maxwell v Cycling New Zealand, ST 11/2024, Sports Tribunal of New Zealand, result decision, 4 July 2024, paras 5–7](https://sportstribunal.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ST-1124-result-no-reasons.pdf)
- [Maxwell v Cycling New Zealand, ST 11/2024, reasons, 11 July 2024, paras 44–48, 59–69](https://www.sportstribunal.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ST1124-reasons.pdf)

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

In this fictional exercise a federation has ranked two eligible riders for one relay nomination. You chair its appeal panel at 14.00. The first-ranked rider challenges a last-minute reversal attributed to ‘combination fit’. The current nominee has paid for travel and has heard of the challenge from a reporter.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Published criteria | The selectors may consider exchange reliability observed in designated relay sessions. A written decision must identify the session and give the rider an opportunity to address materially adverse evidence. |
| Evidence bundle | A selector’s two-line summary attributes a missed exchange to the appellant. The signed session log places a different rider in that pairing. The reserve selector confirms that the video bears a time stamp and is available now. |
| Remedial powers | The exercise rules permit the appeal panel to dismiss, remit or substitute a nomination where remittal is impracticable and the evidence permits a decision. The committee receiving nominations has separate final selection power. |
| Clock and participants | The independent reserve selectors can meet at 16.00. Nominations close at 17.00. Both riders can attend remotely at 14.30. The receiving committee needs 30 minutes to process the nomination. |

## Decisions due

1. Issue directions covering notice to both riders, access to the adverse material, the hearing timetable, and how to handle irrelevant personal information before sharing the bundle.
2. Prepare findings against the actual criterion. Identify what the video and signed log can establish, what requires explanation from a witness, and the threshold for disturbing the decision.
3. Draft operative orders for two outcomes: lawful reconsideration by 16.30 and a failure to assemble a fresh panel in time. Identify the body that retains final selection and who must receive the order.

## An agent assignment for the appeals secretary

Use an agent to assemble the version of the policy in force, the notice, the evidence disclosed and the response deadline. Test it with two equally named policy versions. A panel member must hear the parties, resolve disputed facts and give reasons for the order.

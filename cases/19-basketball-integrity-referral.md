# When two integrity alerts are one source

Cross-department simulation 19 · Chapter 13, *Safeguarding, integrity and welfare* · United States and Canada

## The public record

On 17 April 2024 the NBA announced that it had banned Jontay Porter after a league investigation. It reported that Porter had disclosed confidential information to bettors, limited his participation for betting purposes and bet on NBA games. Licensed operators and a monitoring organisation had drawn attention to suspicious activity. The NBA said it had shared information with federal prosecutors. The announcement identifies several stages in the evidential chain, from an alert, through league fact-finding, to a sporting sanction and external referral.

- [National Basketball Association, decision and account of the investigation, 17 April 2024](https://official.nba.com/jontay-porter-banned-from-nba-for-violating-leagues-gaming-rules/)

## Your brief

The following scenario, roles, records and deadlines are constructed for teaching.

A fictional league integrity officer receives two alerts about the same match. The athlete is due to play again tomorrow. The league’s commercial partner wants reassurance, the club wants selection advice and the welfare officer has heard that family members are being contacted. Work from the simulation records below when deciding how far the case can proceed.

| Exercise record | What you have received |
| --- | --- |
| Alert register | Alert A carries reference 41 and cites an operator’s overnight report. Alert B cites a monitoring bulletin whose footnote also refers to report 41. The underlying report has yet to reach the league. |
| Clinical and employment records | The team clinician recorded a player’s withdrawal before half-time. The welfare lead offers a confidential appointment. Under the league’s exercise policy the independent integrity officer can open a preliminary enquiry. An interim restriction requires the conduct chair’s recorded necessity assessment and a next-day review. |
| Commercial correspondence | The sponsor asks for an individual name and a declaration of innocence before releasing an advertisement. The investigating officer previously advised this sponsor and must declare the connection to the independent chair. |

## Decisions due

1. Produce a provenance diagram for the two alerts and the medical timeline. Mark each inference, specify what could confirm or weaken it, and identify exactly which underlying record to request first.
2. Prepare a preliminary referral for an unconflicted officer, a separate welfare contact and a note to the conduct chair on whether any interim measure is necessary. Give the athlete an opportunity to respond and record a review time.
3. Test a read-only AI agent that groups alerts by source. Supply a pair of duplicate reports and a genuinely independent report as controls. Specify which records the agent may see and who authorises external disclosures.

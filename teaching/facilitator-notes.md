# Facilitator notes

Read after the first decision and the changed circumstances. Award credit for a defensible alternative where the governing authority, evidence and effects on other people support it.

## 01. A federation is suspended. Who can still play?

Record the call, its source and its limits. Seek four written answers covering team entry, event hosting, control of international payments and conditions for restoration. Notify the domestic grant agency within its own deadline. The reversible seat hold preserves a choice if the grant terms permit it. Negotiate a written hotel extension, identify alternative accommodation and escalate any proposal to spend £3,200. The board can authorise expenditure within Harbour’s rules, while the international body controls the entry and host award.

**A defensible alternative.** Declining even the £400 hold is defensible if finance cannot establish that the grant covers it or find an unrestricted budget. Give the chair the possible fare increase and a booking deadline. A board willing to take the hotel risk must explain whose money absorbs a loss, why that risk is proportionate and what it has told families.

- Four distinct powers attributed to their holders
- A funded, authorised travel decision with a cut-off time
- A grant notice and family communication that remain true if entry is refused

## 02. Who gets onto the ballot?

Admit the duly nominated former player. A future skills policy cannot displace the existing rule. Ask each appointing club to confirm its instructions through an independently established contact and follow the governing procedure for competing appointments. Retain originals, obtain the reserve scrutineer’s review of the disputed forms and record whether their resolution could change the result. Explain the ruling to participants without publishing signatures. Put the prospective reform through the member-amendment route and publish reasons and review arrangements for the future nominations committee.

**A defensible alternative.** A temporary adjournment of the count may be justified if the decision-makers have power to adjourn and the conflicting proxy could determine the result. Holding the election first and hearing procedural proposals afterwards may better protect members who relied on the notice. The strongest answer explains who may authorise either route.

- Existing eligibility rule applied to the named candidate
- Individual verification of appointments without disenfranchising valid proxies
- Prospective reform with independent reasons and an athlete voice

## 03. The first Friday after a public venue changes hands

Protect the confirmed Friday contract and secure the facilities manager’s decision independently. Thursday requires a new agreement. The nominal margin on the first offer is S$6,500 before unpriced effects, so record who would pay for accessible replacement transport and who could approve it. Test a shorter rehearsal or a technical setup that leaves the promised school session intact. Follow school users from the invitation through attendance, recording the reason for displacement and what the family had to spend.

**A defensible alternative.** A consensual relocation may make better use of the venue if schools can accept an accessible substitute and the promoter funds every material change, including transport. Show the written acceptance, the cost centre that receives the reimbursement and the official responsible for a complaint or failed journey.

- Confirmed rights distinguished from provisional diary entries
- Net resources and cost-bearing identified across departments
- Access measured for the originally invited users

## 04. What does the coach’s working week establish?

Use the actual rota, payments, approvals and substitution history. A retrospective label has little evidential value about earlier conduct. The founder’s sustained hours and the club’s managerial instructions are important contrasts with the referee record. Ask an adviser to assess the tax periods and employment-rights questions on their respective tests, including the treatment of working time and pay. Ensure amounts already earned are processed through the proper route. Name and check cover for juniors or combine, move or cancel the sessions with timely notice to families.

**A defensible alternative.** The founder may have undertaken materially different engagements in summer and term time. A period-specific review can acknowledge evidence of independent trading without assuming that it determines the terms of the weekly coaching. An interim appointment to cover the juniors is defensible only when the person and safeguarding arrangements are verified.

- Both judicial decisions applied to the right issue
- The actual week tested against the written agreement
- Immediate safe cover and distinct owners for tax and employment review

## 05. A home fixture with six days’ cash

The club has £22,000 unrestricted. After Thursday’s £17,000 payroll it has £5,000 against £11,000 due on Friday, a £6,000 deficit. A cleared £10,000 owner payment would leave £4,000 after both suppliers are paid. A properly authorised £6,000 deferral makes Friday’s balance exactly zero and creates a new liability 14 days later. It leaves no cushion if any unrecorded payment falls due. Advise the board on that risk, notify the league now and ask the away club to hold or release its seats before the deadline. Treat the screenshot and unsettled ticket proceeds separately from bank cash, keep junior funds available for their grant purpose and obtain appropriate insolvency advice when due debts may go unpaid.

**A defensible alternative.** A conditional decision to play can be justified if the signed stadium variation, medical cover, up-to-date commitments and credible means of meeting the deferred payment are verified. A deferral of the fixture may protect supporters and the visiting club where the board cannot demonstrate that position. Do not assume postponement extinguishes debts already contracted.

- £6,000 shortfall and zero-margin deferral correctly traced
- Grant protection and supplier rights survive the fixture decision
- Timed notification reaches players, supporters and the visiting club

## 06. Two clubs agree a fee. Who can move the player?

Keep the sponsor slot separate from registration. The two clubs can settle a fee and the acquiring club can agree a later reporting date. The player must be able to consider the actual terms, including relocation, with her representative. Record any agreed variation and the freely given written consent at execution. If all the requirements are met by 18.00, notify the competition and finance teams before publication. Otherwise close the file as incomplete, retain the existing registration and cancel the announcement. A confidential route to the players’ representative preserves a way to report pressure.

**A defensible alternative.** The acquiring club can withdraw its offer and seek another player, or return in a later window. Keeping this offer open until 18.00 may allow an agreement if the club accepts Monday. Neither choice gives the sponsor power over the deadline or the player.

- Consent is specific, written, voluntary and timed to execution.
- A club decision on reporting date reaches payroll, travel and the registrar.
- An unresolved transfer leaves the player’s existing registration intact at the deadline.

## 07. What does a replacement sponsor actually buy?

On the original numbers the cash contribution after the two quoted direct costs is A$78,000 before any appearance fees, overheads or further rights costs. Cleared receipt of A$30,000 before payroll would leave A$13,000 after that payment, assuming no other outflows. Without the receipt, payroll is short A$17,000. Seek a payment schedule less dependent on final acceptance. Reserve venue advertising to the extent expressly granted by the landlord, and price any alternative only after the production, participation and approval requirements are known. Use staged acceptance and measurable evidence for each right.

**A defensible alternative.** A smaller, enforceable digital package may be preferable to a larger fee that requires six allocations the league cannot grant. The board can also decline and arrange short-term funding, provided finance identifies an authorised source and the cost of losing the offer is recorded.

- Each right has an owner and an identifiable permission or substitute.
- The board can see both the payroll exposure and the cost of fulfilment.
- Community or athlete participation has a cost, a consent route and a delivery record.

## 08. Who answers for admission to a final?

Preserve the relevant source records and ask the operator to describe manual admission and the scope of its timestamp export. The absence of a timestamp is ambiguous. Arrange a separately supported channel for the eleven accounts and for companions. A full-refund cohort costs €21,000, which needs board approval under the exercise mandate. Clubs can contact their own buyers and account to the league for payments in aggregate, with a proportionate exception check. The review should map transfers of responsibility and test what the event owner did to supervise the interfaces.

**A defensible alternative.** An individual claims scheme may fit if the league cannot reliably identify the affected cohort. Provide an assisted application, alternative evidence and review of refusals. A broader refund is also defensible if the records make individual proof unreliable and the board authorises the cost. Keep the separate questions of access improvements and injury complaints open.

- Manual admission and companion bookings remain visible in the evidence design.
- Refund cost and the decision-maker’s authority agree.
- The event owner retains a date and a mechanism for checking corrective work.

## 09. A change of survey reaches the grants committee

Present the two independent survey estimates as observations under different recruitment and collection methods. A population estimate of residents made active by the trust would require a credible comparison and a way to attribute change to the scheme. The register verifies 126 repeaters among 200 entrants, or 63% with a documented return. Anonymous headcounts may conceal further repeats. Make the missing centre data visible, agree a common attendance definition and invite feedback in the formats community partners already use. A conditional extension can fund that repair while preserving access.

**A defensible alternative.** The council could authorise only the existing sessions for one term and reserve expansion funds until coverage improves. A narrower report on activity at the two recording centres may help management if the trust can recover their separate entrant denominators. It cannot supply a district-wide impact estimate.

- Two survey percentages with different collection methods are reported on their own terms.
- 126/200 is identified as documented repeat engagement, with its missing records.
- The funding recommendation has an owner, a review date and an accessible way to hear from non-participants.

## 10. Selected for the team, still waiting to travel

Ask an appropriately qualified adviser to verify the outward, transit and return arrangements privately with the athlete. Record a dated operational conclusion and keep supporting documents available only to staff who need them. Obtain authority for the €690 fare before committing beyond the manager’s limit. A €35 hold preserves an option while a non-refundable €430 booking makes the team bear the clearance risk. Alert the reserve privately to a possible call-up and its deadline. Refer any selection change to the panel, then communicate the confirmed arrangement without disclosing personal history.

**A defensible alternative.** The team can forgo the fare hold and request a new quote when the route is confirmed, recording the risk of a higher fare or no seat. Another defensible plan is an alternative journey approved by the adviser and funded by the proper officer. If travel proves unworkable, a later camp or equivalent coached block may preserve the athlete’s pathway.

- Sporting selection and travel verification have different decision owners.
- The plan protects both the return journey and a realistic opportunity for the reserve.
- Spend authority, personal information and the next contact with the athlete are documented.

## 11. Who decides whether walking is part of golf?

Start with the purpose of the rule, test the claimed effect against the actual event, and record what the individual evidence supports. A private discussion and a confirmed second-day vehicle would make the ruling usable. The field can be told who can request a modification and how competitive conditions are protected. The clinical documentation belongs with the designated decision-makers.

**A defensible alternative.** A revised start time may be workable if the athlete agrees and the draw remains fair. If it changes course conditions materially, weigh an on-time alternative and the modest replacement cost against that disadvantage before confirming the draw.

- Individual assessment tied to the competition rule
- A feasible second-day arrangement
- An explanation the field can use without exposing medical information

## 12. What survived the final whistle in Iraq?

Decide the next evaluation with the associations, state the limits of routine tracking and provide an aggregate account that avoids assigning an attitude to any neighbourhood.

**A defensible alternative.** A phased introduction can supply a comparison where the order is agreed and both waves are measured on the same timetable. If allocation is self-selected, publish the participation pattern and the likely sources of selection alongside any observed differences.

- Distinct denominators and identified missing data
- A workable measure beyond football
- Community oversight of follow-up and publication

## 13. When the person who selects the team hears complaints

Use the conflict bypass immediately. Where a child is in immediate danger, contact emergency services at once. Establish safe supervision, keep a contemporaneous record and pass the concern to the independent lead and appropriate statutory services without waiting for an internal enquiry. Allocate qualified cover and an independent selector. The coach can receive the temporary arrangements and the review process without receiving the child’s confidences during the immediate handover. Explain the limits of confidentiality to the athlete.

**A defensible alternative.** If qualified cover falls through, pause the affected session. The board can ask the independent lead to review previous decisions and present patterns and actions, leaving the underlying safeguarding material with the authorised team.

- Immediate protection and a conflict-free referral
- An interim measure with an owner and review time
- A credible check for retaliation and earlier missed concerns

## 14. A nomination appeal with the Olympic entry deadline approaching

Hear both affected riders on the evidence and address the conflict before choosing a remedy. A properly constituted fresh panel reaching a reasoned conclusion in time can preserve the selectors’ role. If that route is infeasible, identify the rule authorising substitution, the evidence sufficient to determine the particular nomination, and the precise submission deadline. No order should purport to make final selection for the receiving committee.

**A defensible alternative.** If the disputed record cannot support a substituted nomination, seek a lawful time extension or other relief available under the exercise rules, give reasons if the clock precludes it and preserve any further review route. Urgency alone cannot establish which rider satisfied a qualitative criterion.

- Notice and response for both affected riders
- A finding tied to the published criterion and original record
